Chippewa Valley Code Camp 2026 was held on Saturday, March 14th at the [CVTC Business Campus](https://maps.app.goo.gl/7qTFVWdR3vrxCcju9) in Eau Claire.

The day opened with a keynote by [Brian P. Hogan](/speakers/bphogan/), followed by two tracks running side by side: hands-on workshops and presentations.

In the workshop track, [Cory LaNou](/sessions/2026/workshop1/) taught attendees to set up their AI coding assistants to work the way they do, and [Lwin & Min Maung](/sessions/2026/workshop2/) showed how to run a private ChatGPT locally with Docker, Ollama, and Open WebUI.

The presentation track covered a lot of ground: building [multi-language apps in C](/sessions/2026/speakingc/), the UWEC ACM students' [independent search engine](/sessions/2026/search/), [real-time computer vision on the web](/sessions/2026/vision/), [evals and tracing for AI agents](/sessions/2026/agentops/), and an [Avengers-powered golf coaching AI](/sessions/2026/avengers/).

After lunch, five educators faced off in the [IT EDUCATION DEATHMATCH](/sessions/2026/panel/), a gameshow-style panel to decide once and for all which kind of IT education is best.

As always, the event was free to attend thanks to our [2026 sponsors](/sponsors/2026/). We wrapped up the day at the afterparty at Growler Guys.

Thank you to everyone who presented, volunteered, sponsored, and attended. See you in 2027!
